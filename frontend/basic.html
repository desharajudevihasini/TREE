What remains unverified

  • Live EPC lookup requires credentials, address confirmation and an EPC provider response; browser geolocation only suggests a nearby postcode.
  • Installation costs and retrofit-effect assumptions are illustrative, not observed installation outcomes.
  • Value-impact range is a disclosed synthetic sensitivity example, not a causal housing-market estimate.
  • Operational carbon uses published DESNZ 2026 grid-electricity and natural-gas factors; upstream and embodied impacts are excluded. Modelled savings remain unverified.
  • Real NEED model benchmark does not validate custom home's synthetic fallback or retrofit effects.
  • Gemini factuality/groundedness thresholds have not been independently validated.
