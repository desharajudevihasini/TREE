# UK Government NEED 2026 50,000-row anonymised public sample

- Publisher: Department for Energy Security and Net Zero (DESNZ).
- Source: https://www.gov.uk/government/statistics/national-energy-efficiency-data-framework-need-anonymised-data-2026
- Original raw download: https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/6a282ad83b15d05a7ce31fb1/anon2026_50k.csv
- Published: 11 June 2026. Raw source is included unmodified for judges to reproduce the model work.
- Catalogue licensing: UK Open Government Licence (OGL). See https://www.data.gov.uk/dataset/7390402c-e7ce-4e2f-bb08-d8d65f852f47/nationalenergyefficiencydataframeworkanonymiseddata
- **Attribution:** Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0.
- The file is an official *anonymised* public statistical sample, not real Lloyds customer information.
- 2024 energy use is the latest annual target in this dataset; 2026 is the release year, **not** the consumption measurement year.
- Valid meter flags and missing target readings are filtered during model training/evaluation. Scores derived from this data do not demonstrate individual household accuracy or retrofit outcomes.
- Do not combine records with personal addresses or attempt re-identification.

The 50k subset is provided for reproducible analysis. The source page also offers a much larger public sample, but this project does not claim to have evaluated it.
